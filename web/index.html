DogTag Plus

A companion for bulk naming, configuration and auditing of RUCKUS access points, through a SmartZone controller (SZ-100, SZ-144, SZ-300 or any vSZ, version 5 and up) or a RUCKUS One tenant. Everything goes through the controller's own API.

AP Naming and Static Channels

  • Connect. There is no need to pick a zone.
  • Load a CSV file, check the table, and press Run.

Remove Overrides

  • Connect, and load a CSV file of MAC addresses (SmartZone) or serial numbers (RUCKUS One).
  • Choose which per-AP settings to hand back to the zone or venue (the default is the usual set), and whether to switch radios or force DHCP.
  • Press Run.

Channels

  • Connect, pick a zone or venue, and press Refresh to count the connected APs on each channel.

Export

Export APs to CSV writes every AP in the selected zone or venue. Edit it in Excel, Sheets or anything else, and load it back as is under AP Naming.

Command line

The connection can be pre-filled: -ip=, -port=, -user=, -pwd=, and -debug=yes to log every request — the original DogTag's flags all still work. Add -export, -names, -remove-overrides or -channels to run without the console; -h lists everything.
